I am an Assistant Professor in the Multimedia Computing Group in the Intelligent Systems Department at TU Delft. My research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, grounded in graph-structured data.

I lead a research line on the interplay between the transparency and privacy needs of AI and machine learning models. These needs can appear contradictory: understanding a model calls for disclosure, while protecting sensitive information calls for restraint. I see this apparent contradiction as an invitation to understand more deeply what transparency requires, what privacy protects, and where the two can reinforce each other. My recent Vidi project supports my investigation of focused questions about this interplay.

Prior to my being a faculty member at TUD, I was a senior researcher at the L3S Research Centre, and the Leibniz University Hannover. I have also managed several collaborative projects both in Academia and in the industry where I spent a three-year stint after my PhD. I completed my PhD from the Max Planck Institute for Informatics (MPII), in Algorithms and the Complexity group, Saarbruecken, Germany.
